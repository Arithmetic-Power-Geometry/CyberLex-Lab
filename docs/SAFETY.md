# Safety Design

CyberLex Lab is a simulation-only learning environment.

It never:
- collects real passwords or OTPs,
- attacks external systems,
- performs scanning or exploitation,
- generates malware,
- executes password cracking,
- sends payments,
- performs real IP attribution,
- generates deepfakes of students.

Realism comes from synthetic evidence, branching decisions, scoring, time pressure and legal reasoning.
