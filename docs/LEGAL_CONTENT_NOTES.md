# Legal Content Notes

The simulator uses principle-first language. It deliberately uses phrases such as **potentially unlawful**, **lower risk**, and **not enough information** where short facts do not justify a definitive legal conclusion.

The official-resource panel references the Information Technology Act, IT Rules, DPDP framework, other applicable Indian laws depending on facts, cybercrime.gov.in and 1930. These references should be re-verified before major institutional deployment because legal rules and implementation status can change.
